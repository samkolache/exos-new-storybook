## When to use

**Use tabs** to split related content into views people switch between, without leaving the page:

- Parts of one thing, like an order's summary, items and history.
**Don't use them** for steps people go through in order, or for content people need to compare side by side.

### Compared with look-alikes

| Component | Shows | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| **Tabs** | One section at a time | Parts of one thing, over a section |
| [Segmented control](?path=/docs/components-segmented-control--docs) | One view at a time | A compact switch between 2–5 views of the same content, like day, week and month |
| [Accordion](?path=/docs/components-accordion--docs) | Any number, stacked | Sections people scan and open, often on phones |
| [Radio group](?path=/docs/components-forms-radio-group--docs) | Nothing changes until sent | Picking an answer in a form |

## Variants

<DeviceVariation title="Line" of={TabsStories.VarLine}>

Tabs over a section, with a bar under the picked one. Put each tab's content in `<app-tab-panel value="…">` inside `app-tabs`.

For a compact, pill-shaped switch between views, use the [Segmented control](?path=/docs/components-segmented-control--docs).

</DeviceVariation>

## Modifiers

<DeviceVariation title="Count" of={TabsStories.ModCount}>

Add `count` to an item for a number after its label, like 3 items.

</DeviceVariation>

<DeviceVariation title="Disabled tab" of={TabsStories.ModDisabled}>

Add `disabled: true` to an item that has nothing yet. Arrow keys skip it. Hiding it is often clearer.

</DeviceVariation>

## Content

- **One or two words per tab,** nouns: "Summary", "Shipping". Sentence case.
- **Order by use:** most used first.

<DoDont>
  <Guideline type="do" of={TabsStories.DoShortLabels}>use short, parallel labels.</Guideline>
  <Guideline type="dont" of={TabsStories.DontLongLabels}>write long phrases in tabs.</Guideline>
</DoDont>

## Behavior

### Keyboard

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| <kbd>Tab</kbd> | Into the tab list (the picked tab), then into the panel |
| <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd> | Previous or next tab, and shows it; skips disabled tabs; wraps around |
| <kbd>Home</kbd> / <kbd>End</kbd> | First or last tab |

### Mobile and web

| | Mobile (below 672px) | Web (672px and up) |
|---|---|---|
| Line tabs | 48px tall; scroll sideways if they don't fit | 40px tall |
| Many sections | Consider an [Accordion](?path=/docs/components-accordion--docs) instead | Tabs |
| Hover | None | Text darkens |

See [Mobile and web](?path=/docs/foundations-mobile-and-web--docs).

## Accessibility

For the second tab in the live demo, a screen reader reads:

<ScreenReader name="Items 3" role="tab" state="not selected" position="2 of 4" group="Order details" />

- **Roles:** `tablist`, `tab` and `tabpanel`, each panel named by its tab.
- **One Tab stop** for the whole list; arrows move between tabs.
- **Panels are focusable,** so keyboard users can reach content with no links in it.
- **Checked automatically:** every story runs an accessibility scan in the tests, plus keyboard and panel tests.
