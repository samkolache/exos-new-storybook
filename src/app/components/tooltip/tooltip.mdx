## Live demo

Hover or tab to the icon buttons for their names. Hover, tab to or click "list price" for its definition. Click the (i) for a toggletip. On Mobile, icon buttons show no tooltip (phones have no hover), but tapping the term or the (i) works the same.

<DeviceCanvas of={TooltipStories.Overview} defaultDevice="web" />

## When to use

Most tooltips come with another component, like the name of an [Icon button](?path=/docs/components-iconbutton--docs). Use one on its own only where none of those fit.

| | Tooltip | Definition tooltip | Toggletip |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Use it for** | Naming an element that has only an icon | A word people may not know, in a sentence, label or table | Explaining a field, a number or a rule |
| **Opens** | On hover or keyboard focus | On hover, focus, click or tap | On click, tap or Enter |
| **Holds** | One or two words | One sentence | One or two sentences, can include a link |
| **On phones** | Doesn't show (no hover) | Opens on tap | Works the same |

### When not to use

- **For anything people need.** A tooltip disappears when the pointer moves away, and never shows on phones. Put rules people must follow, like a password's length, in [help text](?path=/docs/components-forms-input--docs) that's always on screen.
- **For buttons or links inside it.** People can't tab into a tooltip, so a link in one can't be reached with a keyboard. Use a toggletip.

## Anatomy

<DeviceCanvas of={TooltipStories.Anatomy} noCode />

| # | Part | Shows when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Trigger** | Always. The element the tooltip belongs to: an icon button, or a term with a dotted underline. |
| 2 | **Caret** | With the container. A small point that aims at the middle of the trigger. |
| 3 | **Container** | On hover or focus. Dark, with short text. |

A toggletip has the same idea: an (i) button as its trigger, and a white container under it once opened.

## Variants

<DeviceVariation title="Tooltip" of={TooltipStories.VarTooltip}>

Add `appTooltip="Download"` to any element. It shows after 300ms of hover, or right away on keyboard focus.

- On an icon button, the tooltip repeats its `aria-label`, so add `[tooltipIsLabel]="true"`: screen readers read the name once.

</DeviceVariation>

<DeviceVariation title="Definition tooltip" of={TooltipStories.VarDefinition}>

Wrap the term: `<app-definition-tooltip definition="…">list price</app-definition-tooltip>`. It gets a dotted underline, so people can tell there's more.

- It opens below the term, lined up with its start, so it never covers the line being read.
- A click or tap keeps it open until people click elsewhere or press <kbd>Esc</kbd>, so it works on phones too.

</DeviceVariation>

<DeviceVariation title="Toggletip" of={TooltipStories.VarToggletip}>

`<app-toggletip label="About delivery times">…</app-toggletip>` next to the thing it explains. The text goes between the tags; it can include a link.

</DeviceVariation>

## Modifiers

<DeviceVariation title="Alignment" of={TooltipStories.ModAlignment}>

Line the tooltip up with the **start**, **center** (default) or **end** of the element with `tooltipAlign`. Use start or end near the edge of a page or panel, or so the tooltip doesn't cover something people need. The caret always points at the middle of the element.

</DeviceVariation>

<DeviceVariation title="Placement" of={TooltipStories.ModPlacement}>

Put the tooltip on the **top** (default), **right**, **bottom** or **left** with `tooltipPosition`.

- If there's no room on that side, it flips to the other side, and it slides along to stay on screen.
- Don't let it cover what people are working on, like the field they're typing in.

</DeviceVariation>

<DeviceVariation title="Toggletip next to a field" of={TooltipStories.ModNextToField}>

Put the toggletip after the label, for terms people may not know, like "Security code". Anything people always need belongs in the field's help text instead.

</DeviceVariation>

<DeviceVariation title="Toggletip opening to the left" of={TooltipStories.ModAlignEnd}>

`align="end"` when the (i) is near the right edge, so the toggletip stays on screen.

</DeviceVariation>

## Specs

Use **Mobile / Web** to compare devices. The numbers are measured from the code.

<SpecPictures of={TooltipStories.SpecDrawing} />

- **Tooltip:** white text on dark, up to 240px wide, 8px from the element.
- **Toggletip:** dark text on white with a border, up to 288px wide (the screen width minus 32px on phones). Its (i) button is 24px, with a 44px tap area on phones.

## Content

- **Tooltips:** one or two words that name the action, like "Download". No full stop.
- **Definitions:** a full sentence in sentence case, with a full stop. Say what the word means here, not in general.
- **Toggletips:** one or two sentences. Lead with the answer.
- **Don't repeat** what's already on screen, and never put the only copy of an instruction in any of them.

## Behavior

### States

Closed (the default: hidden) and open. Nothing in a tooltip can be pressed.

### Mouse

- A tooltip opens after the pointer has been on the element for 300ms, so it doesn't flash while the pointer passes over.
- It stays open while the pointer is on the element **or on the tooltip**, so people can move onto it to read it. It closes when the pointer leaves both.
- A definition tooltip also opens on click, and stays open until people click somewhere else.

### Keyboard

| Key | Tooltip | Definition tooltip | Toggletip |
|---|---|---|---|
| <kbd>Tab</kbd> | Focusing the element shows it; leaving hides it | Focusing the term shows it; leaving hides it | To the (i) button; tabbing away closes it |
| <kbd>Enter</kbd> / <kbd>Space</kbd> | — | Keeps it open, or closes it | Opens or closes it |
| <kbd>Esc</kbd> | Hides it; focus stays | Hides it; focus stays | Closes it; focus goes back to the button |

### Mobile and web

| | Mobile (below 672px) | Web (672px and up) |
|---|---|---|
| Tooltip | Doesn't show. Icon buttons still need an `aria-label` | Hover (after 300ms) or keyboard focus |
| Definition tooltip | Tap the term; tap anywhere else to close | Hover, focus or click |
| Toggletip | Tap the (i); tap anywhere else to close | Click, or Enter |
| (i) button | 44px tap area | 24px |
| Toggletip width | Up to the screen width minus 32px | Up to 288px |

See [Mobile and web](?path=/docs/foundations-mobile-and-web--docs).

## Accessibility

For the toggletip in the live demo, a screen reader reads:

<ScreenReader name="About delivery times" role="button" state="collapsed" description="When opened, the text is read out" />

- **Tooltips** use `role="tooltip"` and `aria-describedby`, so they're read after the element's name. With `tooltipIsLabel`, they're skipped, since the name already says it.
- **Definition tooltips** are buttons, so people can reach the term with the keyboard; the definition is read after the term.
- **Toggletips** are buttons with `aria-expanded`; their text sits in a live region, so it's read when it opens.
- **Hoverable and dismissable:** people can move the pointer onto a tooltip without it closing, and <kbd>Esc</kbd> hides all three without moving focus away from what people were on.
- **Checked automatically:** every story runs an accessibility scan in the tests, plus keyboard, hover and definition tests.

## Code

### Tooltip inputs

| Input | Type | Default | Does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `appTooltip` | `string` | `''` | The text |
| `tooltipPosition` | `'top' \| 'right' \| 'bottom' \| 'left'` | `'top'` | Which side it shows on; flips if there's no room |
| `tooltipAlign` | `'start' \| 'center' \| 'end'` | `'center'` | Where it lines up along that side |
| `tooltipIsLabel` | `boolean` | `false` | The text repeats the element's name, so screen readers skip it |

### Definition tooltip inputs

| Input | Type | Default | Does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `definition` | `string` | `''` | The definition, as a sentence |
| `position` | `'top' \| 'right' \| 'bottom' \| 'left'` | `'bottom'` | Which side it shows on |
| `align` | `'start' \| 'center' \| 'end'` | `'start'` | Where it lines up along that side |

The term goes between the tags.
