## When to use

**Use an accordion** when people need only some sections of a long page, and can tell which from the titles:

- FAQs, settings groups, the details of an order.
- On phones, where a long page is tiring to scroll, for content that sits side by side on the web.

**Don't use it** for content everyone needs (show it), or to hide one short paragraph.

### Compared with look-alikes

| Component | Shows | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| **Accordion** | Any number of sections, stacked | Scanning titles and opening what's needed |
| [Tabs](?path=/docs/components-tabs--docs) | One view at a time | Switching between parts of one thing |
| [Dropdown card](?path=/docs/components-dropdown-card--docs) | One card with its answers | A summary that opens to show detail |

## Anatomy

<DeviceCanvas of={AccordionStories.Anatomy} noCode />

| # | Part | Shows when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Title** | Always. A heading, so it's in the page outline. |
| 2 | **Meta** | Optional. A short count or status at the end of the row. |
| 3 | **Chevron** | Always. Points down when closed, up when open. |
| 4 | **Content** | When open. Anything: text, fields, lists. |
| 5 | **Row** | Always. The whole row opens and closes the section. |

## Variants

<DeviceVariation title="Several open" of={AccordionStories.VarMultiple}>

The default: people open as many sections as they like. Best when they compare sections.

</DeviceVariation>

<DeviceVariation title="One at a time" of={AccordionStories.VarSingle}>

`[multiple]="false"`: opening a section closes the open one. Keeps the page short on phones.

</DeviceVariation>

## Size

<DeviceVariation title="Small, medium, large" of={AccordionStories.SizeSwitchable} sizeSwitch>

Row height on the web. On phones, rows are always at least 44px. Use the switch in the toolbar.

- **Small** (`sm`, 32px): dense side panels.
- **Medium** (`md`, 40px, default): most pages.
- **Large** (`lg`, 48px): a page that's mostly the accordion, like an FAQ.

</DeviceVariation>

## Modifiers

<DeviceVariation title="Meta and disabled" of={AccordionStories.ModMeta}>

`meta` adds a short count or status at the end of the row, like "12". `[disabled]="true"` on an item that has nothing yet.

</DeviceVariation>

<DeviceVariation title="Chevron at the start" of={AccordionStories.ModChevronStart}>

`chevron="start"` for lists inside other content, like a side panel, so the chevron lines up with what's above.

</DeviceVariation>

## Specs

| | Web | Mobile |
|---|---|---|
| Row height | 32 / 40 / 48px | At least 44px |
| Title | 14 or 16px, medium | 16px, medium |
| Chevron | 16px, turns over 0.2s | Same |
| Dividers | 1px between rows, and above and below | Same |
| Content padding | 4px top, 16px bottom | Same |

## Content

- **Titles say what's inside,** so people can skip what they don't need: "Returns", not "More information".
- **Keep what everyone needs outside,** like a total or a deadline.
- **Order sections by how often they're needed.**

<DoDont>
  <Guideline type="do" of={AccordionStories.DoScannableTitles}>write titles people can scan.</Guideline>
  <Guideline type="dont" of={AccordionStories.DontVagueTitles}>use vague titles like "Details".</Guideline>
  <Guideline type="do" of={AccordionStories.DoShowWhatMatters}>show what everyone needs, and fold the rest.</Guideline>
  <Guideline type="dont" of={AccordionStories.DontHideWhatMatters}>hide key information in a section.</Guideline>
</DoDont>

## Behavior

### Keyboard

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| <kbd>Tab</kbd> | To the next row, then into open content |
| <kbd>Enter</kbd> / <kbd>Space</kbd> | Opens or closes the focused section |

### Mobile and web

| | Mobile (below 672px) | Web (672px and up) |
|---|---|---|
| Row height | At least 44px | 32, 40 or 48px |
| Use | Often instead of side-by-side columns or tabs | When people need only some sections |
| Hover | None | Light grey row |

See [Mobile and web](?path=/docs/foundations-mobile-and-web--docs).

## Accessibility

For the first section in the live demo, a screen reader reads:

<ScreenReader name="Shipping" role="button" state="collapsed" group="heading level 3" />

- **Each title is a heading** (`headingLevel`, 3 by default) with a button inside, so people can jump between sections.
- **The button says if it's open** with `aria-expanded`, and controls a named region.
- **Checked automatically:** every story runs an accessibility scan in the tests, plus open, close and one-at-a-time tests.
