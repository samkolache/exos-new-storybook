## When to use

**Use a notification** to tell people about something they didn't ask to see, or the result of something they did:

- The result of an action, like "Order placed" or "Couldn't save".
- Something about the whole page, like "New version available".
- Something that needs attention soon, like an expiring card.

### Compared with look-alikes

| Component | Blocks the page | Stays | Use it for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Inline notification** | No | Until fixed or dismissed | Something about this page or section |
| **Toast** | No | A few seconds (info, success) | A quick result: "Saved", "Order archived" |
| [Modal](?path=/docs/components-modal--docs) | Yes | Until people choose | A decision before going on: "Delete photo?" |
| Field message ([Input](?path=/docs/components-forms-input--docs)) | No | Until fixed | A problem with one field |

Pick the kind by what happened. Use the switch in the toolbar; only `kind` changes in the code.

- **Info:** good to know, nothing to do.
- **Success:** it worked.
- **Warning:** it worked, or will soon matter; check something.
- **Error:** it didn't work. Say what to do next.

</DeviceVariation>

<DeviceVariation title="Toast" of={NotificationStories.VarToast} noCode>

A notification that floats above the page for a moment, for the result of something people just did. Try the buttons, then switch to Mobile.

- **Web:** top right. **Phones:** along the bottom, where thumbs are, clear of the home bar.
- Info and success hide after 6 seconds; hovering or focusing one keeps it open.
- Errors, warnings and toasts with an action stay until dismissed, so nobody misses them.
- At most 3 at a time; the newest is closest to the edge.

```ts
// Once, in the app shell:      <app-toast-region />
// Anywhere:
inject(ToastService).show({ kind: 'success', title: 'Changes saved' });
inject(ToastService).show({ kind: 'info', title: 'Order archived', actionLabel: 'Undo', onAction: () => restore() });
```

</DeviceVariation>

## Modifiers

<DeviceVariation title="Action" of={NotificationStories.ModAction}>

`actionLabel` adds one action, like "Try again" or "View order"; it emits `action`. One at most: a notification isn't a menu.

</DeviceVariation>

<DeviceVariation title="Dismissible" of={NotificationStories.ModDismissible}>

`[dismissible]="true"` adds a close button that emits `dismissed`; you remove the notification. Leave errors people must fix undismissible: they go away once fixed.

</DeviceVariation>

<DeviceVariation title="Title only" of={NotificationStories.ModTitleOnly}>

Leave out `message` when the title says it all, like "Changes saved".

</DeviceVariation>

## Content

- **Title: what happened,** in a few words: "Payment failed", "Order placed". Not "Error" or "Success".
- **Message: what to do,** in one or two sentences, without blaming people.
- **Action: a verb,** like "Try again" or "Update card".
- **Write for when it's read:** "Saved", not "Saving was successful".

<DoDont>
  <Guideline type="do" of={NotificationStories.DoSaySolution}>say what happened and what to do.</Guideline>
  <Guideline type="dont" of={NotificationStories.DontVague}>write "Something went wrong" and stop there.</Guideline>
  <Guideline type="do" of={NotificationStories.DoInlineForBlocking}>keep problems people must fix inline, where they'll stay.</Guideline>
  <Guideline type="dont" of={NotificationStories.DontToastForBlocking}>put a problem in a toast that can disappear.</Guideline>
  <Guideline type="do" of={NotificationStories.DoOneAtATime}>sum several issues up in one notification.</Guideline>
  <Guideline type="dont" of={NotificationStories.DontStack}>stack several notifications. People stop reading them.</Guideline>
</DoDont>

## Behavior

### Keyboard

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| <kbd>Tab</kbd> | To the action, then the close button |
| <kbd>Enter</kbd> / <kbd>Space</kbd> | Presses the focused button |

A notification never takes focus when it appears, so it doesn't pull people away from what they're doing. Screen readers announce it instead.

### Mobile and web

| | Mobile (below 672px) | Web (672px and up) |
|---|---|---|
| Toasts | Along the bottom, in thumb reach | Top right |
| Toast width | Full width, 16px from each side (in line with the page content) | 384px |
| Close and action | 44px tap areas | Their own size |
| Hover | None (toasts don't pause) | Hover pauses a toast |

See [Mobile and web](?path=/docs/foundations-mobile-and-web--docs).

## Accessibility

For the payment error in the live demo, a screen reader announces as soon as it appears:

<ScreenReader name="Payment failed. Your card was declined. Try another card." role="alert" state="announced right away" />

- **Errors and warnings** use `role="alert"`, so they're read right away. **Info and success** use `role="status"`, read when the screen reader is free.
- **Not by colour alone:** each kind has its own icon shape, and the title says what happened.
- **Time to read:** toasts that matter stay until dismissed, and hovering or focusing pauses the rest.
- **Close buttons say what they close:** "Dismiss: Payment failed".
- **Checked automatically:** every notification story runs an accessibility scan in the tests, plus role, button and toast tests.
