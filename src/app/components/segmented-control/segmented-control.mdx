## Live demo

A photo gallery that switches between its sections, on a phone and on the web. Pick a segment: the photo and the thumb under the picked segment change together.

<DeviceCanvas of={SegmentedStories.MobileExample} webOf={SegmentedStories.WebExample} defaultDevice="mobile" />

## When to use

**Use a segmented control** to switch the view of what's already on screen:

- Sections of one collection, like exterior and interior photos.
- Ranges of the same data, like day, week and month.
- Ways to show the same list, like a list or a map.

**Don't use it** for actions (use buttons), for answers in a form (use a radio group or chips), or for more than 5 options.

### Compared with look-alikes

| Component | People | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| **Segmented control** | Switch the view, right away | 2 to 5 views of the same content |
| [Tabs](?path=/docs/components-tabs--docs) | Switch between sections of a page | Parts of one thing, like an order's summary and history; any number |
| [Radio group](?path=/docs/components-forms-radio-group--docs) | Pick an answer that's sent later | Answers in a form |
| [Chip](?path=/docs/components-chip--docs) | Pick one or several short answers | Answers and filters |

If nothing on screen changes when people pick, it's an answer, not a view: use a radio group or chips.

## Variants

Two variants, picked by what's behind the control.

<DeviceVariation title="Contrast" of={SegmentedStories.VarContrast}>

The default: a white track with a dark thumb. It stands out over photos, maps and busy screens, like at the bottom of a gallery.

- No `appearance` needed.
- Floats on a soft shadow, so it reads over any background.

</DeviceVariation>

<DeviceVariation title="Subtle" of={SegmentedStories.VarSubtle}>

A grey track with a white thumb. Quieter, for inside cards, panels and toolbars where the contrast look would compete with the content.

- `appearance="subtle"`.

</DeviceVariation>

## Size

<DeviceVariation title="Small, medium, large" of={SegmentedStories.Switchable} sizeSwitch>

Use the switch in the toolbar. Only `size` changes in the code.

- **Small** (`sm`, 32px): dense web screens like toolbars and table headers.
- **Medium** (`md`, 40px, default): most screens. Lines up with medium inputs and buttons.
- **Large** (`lg`, 48px, 16px text): over photos and media, like a gallery.

Every size has a 4px track and 16px of padding on each side of a segment. On phones the control is **48px** tall (**44px** small) and full width. Switch the toolbar to Mobile to see it.

</DeviceVariation>

## Modifiers

<DeviceVariation title="Count" of={SegmentedStories.ModCount}>

Add `count` to an item for short text after its label, like a total (`12`) or progress (`'4/6'`).

Add `countLabel` to say what the count means to screen readers, like `'4 of 6 photos'`. Otherwise "4/6" is read as "4 slash 6".

</DeviceVariation>

<DeviceVariation title="Full width" of={SegmentedStories.ModFullWidth}>

`[fullWidth]="true"` stretches the control across its container; segments stay equal. On phones it's always full width.

</DeviceVariation>

<DeviceVariation title="Disabled segment" of={SegmentedStories.ModDisabledSegment}>

Add `disabled: true` to an item with nothing to show yet, like a floor plan that hasn't been uploaded. Arrow keys skip it. If it will never have content, leave it out.

</DeviceVariation>

## Content

- **One or two words per segment,** nouns, in sentence case: "Exterior", "Week".
- **Parallel labels** of about the same length. Segments are always equal widths, so the longest label sets the width.
- **2 to 5 segments.** More than 5 won't fit on a phone.
- **Counts are short:** a number, or progress like 4/6.

<DoDont>
  <Guideline type="do" of={SegmentedStories.DoShortLabels}>use one or two words per segment.</Guideline>
  <Guideline type="dont" of={SegmentedStories.DontLongLabels}>write phrases. They're cut off on phones.</Guideline>
  <Guideline type="do" of={SegmentedStories.DoFewSegments}>offer 2 to 5 segments.</Guideline>
  <Guideline type="dont" of={SegmentedStories.DontManySegments}>squeeze in more. Use tabs or a select.</Guideline>
  <Guideline type="do" of={SegmentedStories.DoViews}>switch between views of the same content.</Guideline>
  <Guideline type="dont" of={SegmentedStories.DontActions}>use segments for actions. Use buttons.</Guideline>
</DoDont>

## States

<DeviceVariation title="All states" of={SegmentedStories.AllStates} noCode>

Both variants, with a disabled segment and fully disabled.

- **Picked:** on the thumb. Always exactly one.
- **Not picked:** on the track. On web, hovering darkens it (and fills it, on contrast).
- **Focused:** a blue ring, from the keyboard only.
- **Disabled segment:** grey text, skipped by the arrow keys.
- **Disabled control:** every segment grey and the thumb turns grey. Explain nearby why.

</DeviceVariation>

## Behavior

### Keyboard

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| <kbd>Tab</kbd> | Into the control, on the picked segment. One Tab stop for the whole control |
| <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd> | Previous or next segment, and picks it; skips disabled ones; wraps around |
| <kbd>Home</kbd> / <kbd>End</kbd> | First or last segment |

### Picking

- **The view changes right away:** there's nothing to confirm. Listen to `value` (or `valueChange`) and change the view.
- **The thumb slides** to the picked segment in 240ms. With reduce motion turned on, it moves without sliding.
- **Keep the pick** when people come back to the screen, so they don't have to pick again.

### Mobile and web

See [Mobile and web](?path=/docs/foundations-mobile-and-web--docs) for the rules every component follows.

| | Mobile (below 672px) | Web (672px and up) |
|---|---|---|
| Width | Full width | As wide as its segments, or full width with `fullWidth` |
| Height | 44 / 48 / 48px | 32 / 40 / 48px |
| Segments | Equal widths | Equal widths |
| Hover | None | Text darkens; contrast segments fill grey |

## Accessibility

For the Exterior segment in the playground, a screen reader reads:

<ScreenReader name="Exterior, 4 of 6 photos" role="tab" state="not selected" position="1 of 2" group="Photos" />

- **Roles:** `tablist` and `tab`. Set `controls` on an item to point it at the region it shows.
- **Name the control** with `label`, like "Photos".
- **Counts** are read with `countLabel` when the short form isn't clear out loud.
- **Colour is never the only signal:** the picked segment sits on the thumb, and screen readers hear "selected".
- **Contrast:** labels and counts pass 4.5:1 on both tracks and the thumb.
- **Checked automatically:** every story runs an accessibility scan in the tests, plus size, keyboard, click and disabled tests.
