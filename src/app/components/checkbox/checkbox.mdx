## When to use

**Use checkboxes** when:

- People can pick **any number** of options, including none.
- One option is a yes or no, like "Remember me" or agreeing to terms.
- A parent option controls a list, like "All updates".

### Compared with look-alikes

| Component | People pick | Takes effect | Use it for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Checkbox** | Any number | When the form is sent | Several answers, or one yes/no in a form |
| [Radio group](?path=/docs/components-forms-radio-group--docs) | Exactly one | When the form is sent | One answer worth comparing |
| [Chip](?path=/docs/components-chip--docs) (multi) | Any number | When the form is sent | Short answers on one line |
| **Switch** (below) | On or off | Right away | Settings, like dark mode |

## Variants

The same component, decided by `options`.

<DeviceVariation title="Single" of={CheckboxStories.VarSingle}>

One yes/no. No `options`: `label` is the checkbox's own label and `checked` holds the answer.

- Use `[(checked)]`, or a reactive form with a boolean.

</DeviceVariation>

<DeviceVariation title="Group" of={CheckboxStories.VarGroup}>

A question with several answers. Pass `options`; `label` becomes the question and `value` is a string array, in the order of `options`.

</DeviceVariation>

<DeviceVariation title="Switch" of={CheckboxStories.VarSwitch}>

A single on/off **setting that takes effect right away**, like dark mode. Add `appearance="switch"`; it's announced as a switch.

- On web it sits before its label.
- On phones it moves to the end of the row at 51 × 31, like a phone's settings list. Switch the toolbar to Mobile to see it.
- For answers that are sent with a form, keep a checkbox.

</DeviceVariation>

## Modifiers

One input each.

<DeviceVariation title="Select all" of={CheckboxStories.ModSelectAll}>

Add `selectAll="All updates"` for a parent checkbox above the options, which are indented under it.

- It shows a dash when some are picked, and a check when all are.
- Clicking it picks every option, or clears them if all are picked. Disabled options keep their state.

</DeviceVariation>

<DeviceVariation title="Description" of={CheckboxStories.ModDescription}>

Add `description` to an option for one short line of detail. It's read by screen readers after the option's name.

</DeviceVariation>

<DeviceVariation title="Help text (single)" of={CheckboxStories.ModSingleHelp}>

On a single checkbox, `helpText` sits under its label.

</DeviceVariation>

<DeviceVariation title="Horizontal" of={CheckboxStories.ModHorizontal}>

Add `[inline]="true"` for 2 or 3 short options side by side. On phones they always stack. Vertical stays the default, since it's easier to scan.

</DeviceVariation>

<DeviceVariation title="AI suggested" of={CheckboxStories.ModAI}>

Add `suggested: true` to the options the AI picked, and put them in `value`.

- Each gets a sparkle, and the group label gets an **AI label**.
- Set `aiExplanation` to say why, in one sentence.

</DeviceVariation>

<DeviceVariation title="AI suggested, changed" of={CheckboxStories.ModAIChanged}>

Once people change the AI's picks, the AI label becomes a **revert** button that puts them back. Nothing to set: it follows from `value`.

</DeviceVariation>

## Content

- **Write labels as positive statements:** "Email me about offers", not "Don't email me".
- **Keep option labels to 1 to 3 words,** sentence case. Put detail in `description`.
- **Start unchecked,** especially for anything that sends people things or costs them money. They should opt in.
- **For terms, say what they agree to** and link the documents next to the checkbox.

<DoDont>
  <Guideline type="do" of={CheckboxStories.DoPositive}>write what checking it does.</Guideline>
  <Guideline type="dont" of={CheckboxStories.DontNegative}>use negatives. People have to think twice.</Guideline>
  <Guideline type="do" of={CheckboxStories.DoCheckboxForMany}>use checkboxes when several answers can be true.</Guideline>
  <Guideline type="dont" of={CheckboxStories.DontCheckboxForOne}>use them when only one can be. Use radios.</Guideline>
  <Guideline type="do" of={CheckboxStories.DoSwitchInstant}>use switches for settings that change right away.</Guideline>
  <Guideline type="dont" of={CheckboxStories.DontSwitchInForm}>use switches for form answers. Nothing happens until people send.</Guideline>
  <Guideline type="do" of={CheckboxStories.DoUnchecked}>let people opt in.</Guideline>
  <Guideline type="dont" of={CheckboxStories.DontPrechecked}>check marketing for them.</Guideline>
</DoDont>

## States

<DeviceVariation title="All states" of={CheckboxStories.AllStates} noCode>

- **Warning:** the picks are allowed but worth a second look.
- **Invalid:** a required checkbox isn't checked, or a group needs at least one. Says how to fix it.
- **Read only:** grey outlines with a dark check. People can Tab to it and read it but not change it.
- **Disabled:** can't be used right now, and is skipped by <kbd>Tab</kbd>. Say nearby why.

Warning and invalid messages lead with an icon, so colour is never the only signal. The AI label stays active even when the group is disabled or read only.

</DeviceVariation>

## Behavior

### Keyboard

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| <kbd>Tab</kbd> / <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>Tab</kbd> | To each checkbox in turn, including Select all |
| <kbd>Space</kbd> | Turns the focused checkbox on or off |
| <kbd>Enter</kbd> / <kbd>Esc</kbd> on the AI label | Opens / closes the explanation |

A group's `value` follows the order of `options`, not the order people clicked.

### Mobile and web

| | Mobile (below 672px) | Web (672px and up) |
|---|---|---|
| Row height | 44px, the whole row is the target | 32px |
| Horizontal | Stacks | Side by side |
| Switch | 51 × 31px, at the end of the row | 48 × 24px, before the label |
| AI label and revert | 44px tap areas | Their own size |
| Hover | None | Pointer on the row |

See [Mobile and web](?path=/docs/foundations-mobile-and-web--docs).

## Accessibility

For the required terms checkbox after a failed send, a screen reader reads:

<ScreenReader name="I agree to the terms and privacy policy" role="checkbox" state="not checked, required, invalid entry" description="Agree to the terms to continue." />

- **Groups** are a `fieldset` named by the group label. Messages and help text describe the group.
- **Select all** is a real checkbox in the mixed state, with `aria-controls` pointing at the options it controls.
- **Read only** uses `aria-readonly`, and clicks and Space are ignored.
- **AI:** suggested options' names end in "(AI suggested)". The AI label is a button whose explanation is announced when it opens.
- **Checked automatically:** every checkbox story runs an accessibility scan in the tests, plus keyboard, select all, read-only, AI revert and screen reader tests.
