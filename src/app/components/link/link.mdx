## When to use

**Use a link** to go somewhere: another page, a section, a file or another site.

### Compared with look-alikes

| Component | Does | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| **Link** | Goes somewhere | "View your orders", "Terms of service" |
| [Button](?path=/docs/components-button--docs) | Does something here | "Save", "Delete order", "Send" |
| [Dropdown](?path=/docs/components-dropdown--docs) | Opens a menu of actions | "More actions" |

If it changes something (saves, deletes, sends, opens a modal), it's a button, even if it looks like text.

## Variants

Two variants, picked by where the link sits.

<DeviceVariation title="Inline" of={LinkStories.VarInline}>

Inside a sentence. Always underlined, so it stands out from the text around it by more than colour. Takes the size of its text.

</DeviceVariation>

<DeviceVariation title="Standalone" of={LinkStories.VarStandalone}>

On its own, like "View your orders" under a card. Add `[standalone]="true"`.

- Medium weight and no underline until hover: its place on the page already says it's a link.
- On phones it gets a 44px tap area.

</DeviceVariation>

## Size

<DeviceVariation title="Small, medium, large" of={LinkStories.SizeSwitchable} sizeSwitch>

Standalone links only; inline links match their text. Use the switch in the toolbar.

- **Small** (`sm`, 14px): under cards and in dense lists.
- **Medium** (`md`, 16px, default): most places.
- **Large** (`lg`, 18px): next to large text, like a page intro.

</DeviceVariation>

## Modifiers

<DeviceVariation title="External" of={LinkStories.ModExternal}>

Add `[external]="true"` for links to other sites. It opens in a new tab, shows an icon, and tells screen readers "(opens in a new tab)". Links inside your product open in the same tab.

</DeviceVariation>

<DeviceVariation title="Disabled" of={LinkStories.ModDisabled}>

Add `[disabled]="true"` when the destination isn't ready, like an invoice still being made. It's grey, can't be clicked and is skipped by <kbd>Tab</kbd>. Say nearby why, or hide the link instead.

</DeviceVariation>

## Content

- **Say where it goes:** "View your orders", "Return policy". People scanning a page, and screen reader users jumping between links, read link text out of context.
- **Never "click here" or "read more"** on their own.
- **Link the words that describe the destination,** not a whole sentence.
- **Keep the same text for the same destination** across the product.

<DoDont>
  <Guideline type="do" of={LinkStories.DoDescriptive}>link the words that say where it goes.</Guideline>
  <Guideline type="dont" of={LinkStories.DontClickHere}>write "click here". It means nothing out of context.</Guideline>
  <Guideline type="do" of={LinkStories.DoLinkToGo}>use links to go somewhere.</Guideline>
  <Guideline type="dont" of={LinkStories.DontLinkToAct}>use links for actions like delete. Use a button.</Guideline>
</DoDont>

## States

<DeviceVariation title="All states" of={LinkStories.AllStates} noCode>

Hover darkens the colour and thickens the underline (web only). Focus adds a blue outline; tab to any link above to see it.

</DeviceVariation>

## Behavior

### Keyboard

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| <kbd>Tab</kbd> | To the next link |
| <kbd>Enter</kbd> | Follows the link |

Links don't respond to <kbd>Space</kbd>; buttons do. That's one reason actions belong on buttons.

### Mobile and web

| | Mobile (below 672px) | Web (672px and up) |
|---|---|---|
| Standalone tap area | At least 44px tall | The text |
| Hover | None | Darker, thicker underline |
| External links | New tab; the phone shows a back button to return | New tab |

See [Mobile and web](?path=/docs/foundations-mobile-and-web--docs).

### Edge cases

<SpecimenGrid>
  <Specimen of={LinkStories.EdgeLongLink}>**Long link:** wraps with the text; the underline follows every line.</Specimen>
  <Specimen of={LinkStories.EdgeNextToText}>**Two links close together:** keep a few words between them so they're easy to tap.</Specimen>
</SpecimenGrid>

## Accessibility

For the external link in the live demo, a screen reader reads:

<ScreenReader name="Shipping rates (opens in a new tab)" role="link" state="no state" />

- **Not by colour alone:** inline links are always underlined.
- **New tabs are announced,** so people aren't surprised by a missing back history.
- **Disabled links** have no `href`, so they can't be followed or focused, and are announced as unavailable.
- **Checked automatically:** every link story runs an accessibility scan in the tests.
