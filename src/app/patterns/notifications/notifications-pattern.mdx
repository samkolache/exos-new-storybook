# Notifications

How to tell people something: which component, how loud, and for how long. Use the **quietest one that works**. Every interruption costs attention.

## From quietest to loudest

<DeviceCanvas of={Stories.Ladder} defaultDevice="mobile" noCode />

| | Use it for | Stays | Blocks |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Field message** ([Input](?path=/docs/components-forms-input--docs)) | A problem with one answer | Until fixed | No |
| **2. Inline notification** ([Notification](?path=/docs/components-notification--docs)) | Something about this page or section, or several problems at once | Until fixed or dismissed | No |
| **3. Toast** ([Notification](?path=/docs/components-notification--docs)) | The result of something people just did | A few seconds (info, success) | No |
| **4. Modal** ([Modal](?path=/docs/components-modal--docs)) | A decision people must make before going on | Until they choose | Yes |

## Which one

| Situation | Use |
|---|---|
| An email address is wrong | Field message |
| A form was sent with problems | Field messages, plus an error summary at the top (see [Forms](?path=/docs/patterns-forms--docs)) |
| Saved, archived, sent | Success toast; add "Undo" when it can be undone |
| Couldn't save because the connection dropped | Error toast that stays, with "Try again" |
| A card expires next week | Inline warning on the billing page |
| A new version is available | Info notification at the top of the page, dismissible |
| Delete something that can't be undone | Modal, with "Delete" and "Cancel" |
| A list couldn't load | [Empty state](?path=/docs/patterns-empty-states--docs) error in place of the list |

## Kinds

| Kind | Means | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Info** | Good to know | "New version available" |
| **Success** | It worked | "Order placed" |
| **Warning** | It worked, but check something, or something will matter soon | "Card expires soon" |
| **Error** | It didn't work; here's what to do | "Payment failed. Try another card." |

## Rules

- **Next to what it's about.** A field's problem goes under the field; a page's problem at the top of the page.
- **One at a time.** Several notifications stacked get ignored. Sum them up: "3 items need your attention".
- **Things people must act on don't disappear.** Errors, warnings and toasts with an action stay until dismissed.
- **Never only colour.** Every kind has its own icon, and the title says what happened.
- **Don't confirm what people can see.** If the page already shows the change, skip the toast.

## Mobile and web

| | Mobile (below 672px) | Web (672px and up) |
|---|---|---|
| Toasts | Along the bottom, in thumb reach, clear of the home bar | Top right |
| Modals | A sheet from the bottom | Centred |
| Inline notifications | Full width | As wide as the content they're about |

## Accessibility

- **Errors and warnings are announced right away;** info and success when the screen reader is free.
- **Notifications don't take focus,** except the error summary on a form and modals, which people must deal with.
- **Toasts that matter stay** until dismissed, and hovering or focusing pauses the others.
