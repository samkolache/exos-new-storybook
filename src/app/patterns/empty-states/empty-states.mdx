# Empty states

What to show where content would be, when there isn't any. An empty screen should say **why it's empty** and **what to do next**, never just "No data".

## Three kinds

Each has its own icon and its own job. Use `<app-empty-state>` with the matching `kind`.

### First use: nothing here yet

People haven't added anything. Explain what will appear here, and give one way to start.

<DeviceCanvas of={Stories.FirstUse} defaultDevice="mobile" />

### No results: a search or filter found nothing

Say what was searched, suggest a fix, and offer a way back, like clearing filters.

<DeviceCanvas of={Stories.NoResults} defaultDevice="mobile" />

### Error: it couldn't load

Say what didn't load, what people can do, and offer "Try again". It's announced to screen readers.

<DeviceCanvas of={Stories.LoadError} defaultDevice="mobile" />

### Inside cards and panels

`[compact]="true"` for a small area, like an empty list inside a card.

<DeviceCanvas of={Stories.Compact} defaultDevice="web" />

## Content

| | Title | Message | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| **First use** | What's missing: "No orders yet" | What will be here | Start: "Start shopping" |
| **No results** | What was searched: "No results for 'lamp'" | How to find it | Go back: "Clear filters" |
| **Error** | What failed: "Couldn't load your orders" | Why, if known, and what to try | "Try again" |

- **One action at most.** The primary button for first use; a secondary one otherwise.
- **No jokes on errors.** People are already stuck.

<DoDont>
  <Guideline type="do" of={Stories.DoHelpful}>say why it's empty and what to do.</Guideline>
  <Guideline type="dont" of={Stories.DontBlank}>show "No data" and nothing else.</Guideline>
</DoDont>

## Mobile and web

| | Mobile (below 672px) | Web (672px and up) |
|---|---|---|
| Where | In the middle of the empty area, below any header and filters | Same |
| Width | Full width, 16px from the edges | Up to 416px, centred |

Keep the page's header, search and filters visible, so people can change what they asked for.

## Accessibility

- **Error states are announced** (`role="alert"`). First use and no results are read in place.
- **The icon is decorative;** the title says what happened.
- **Checked automatically:** every example runs an accessibility scan, plus an action test.
