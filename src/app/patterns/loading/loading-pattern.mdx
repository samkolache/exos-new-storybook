# Loading

How to show that something is happening, so waiting feels short and nobody wonders if it's broken. The pieces are on the [Loading](?path=/docs/components-loading--docs) component page; this page says which to use when.

## Pick by how long, and how much

| Wait | What's loading | Show |
|---|---|---|
| Under 1 second | Anything | **Nothing.** A flash of a spinner feels slower than none. |
| 1 to 10 seconds | A whole page or list | A **large spinner** where the content goes |
| 1 to 10 seconds | Part of a page | A **spinner overlay** on that part; the rest stays usable |
| 1 to 10 seconds | The result of a button | **Inline loading** next to it: "Saving…" then "Saved" |
| Over 10 seconds | A task you can measure | A **progress bar** with a value, and let people keep working |
| Over 10 seconds | A task you can't measure | A **moving progress bar** and a time estimate, if you have one |

## Rules

- **One loading indicator per area.** Several spinners at once look broken.
- **Say what's loading:** "Loading your orders", not "Please wait".
- **Don't block what doesn't need blocking.** Load parts of a page on their own.
- **When it fails,** replace the spinner with an [empty state](?path=/docs/patterns-empty-states--docs) error and "Try again". Never spin forever: give up after about 30 seconds.
- **Respect reduce motion.** The components slow down or stop moving when people ask for less motion.

## Mobile and web

| | Mobile (below 672px) | Web (672px and up) |
|---|---|---|
| Page load | Large spinner in the middle of the screen; keep the top bar | Large spinner in the content area; keep the side navigation |
| Slow connections | More common: load the most important part first | Less common |
| Long tasks | Keep going if people switch apps, and notify them when done | Keep going in another tab |

## Accessibility

- **Status messages** (spinners and inline loading) are announced without moving focus.
- **Progress bars** expose their value to screen readers.
- **Don't move focus** while loading; when content arrives, leave focus where it was.
